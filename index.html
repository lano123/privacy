Privacy Policy

Double Progression · last updated 7 October 2026

Double Progression does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours that your training is sent to. Everything the app records stays on your phone unless you yourself switch on a backup. The one exception is buying Plus: the purchase is processed by Google Play and verified by RevenueCat — see below.

This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device and what does not. It covers the Double Progression mobile app for Android and iOS, published by Martin Lancarič.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your phone's private app storage. It is not transmitted anywhere, it is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you uninstall Double Progression.

What can leave your device — and only if you ask

Backup to your own Google Drive

Off by default. If you turn it on, the app signs you in with Google and writes a copy of your training data to your own Google Drive, into the hidden application folder (appDataFolder).

  • The scope requested is drive.appdata — the narrowest Google Drive offers. The app cannot see, list or read any other file in your Drive.
  • The backup is in your Drive, under your Google account. We have no server and no copy of it. We cannot read it.
  • Your Google e-mail address is shown in the app so you know which account is connected. It is held in memory only and is not stored or transmitted to us.
  • You can disconnect at any time, and delete the backup from your Google account's app-data settings.

A backup made entirely into your own cloud storage, which the developer cannot access, is not collection of your data by us — and it is not declared as such in the Google Play Data safety section.

Buying Plus (in-app purchases)

Only if you open the Plus screen, buy or restore a purchase, or have an active Plus subscription. Anyone who never does any of this never contacts these services.

  • Google Play handles the payment. Your card and billing details go to Google only; we never see them. Google's own privacy policy applies.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) verifies purchases and subscriptions for us, because the app has no server of its own. It receives a random anonymous ID created by the app (not your name, e-mail or Google account), your purchase and subscription details (product, price, currency, dates, order ID), basic technical information (app version, Android version, store country and language) and, as with any connection, your IP address.
  • If you have a subscription, the app checks with RevenueCat when it starts whether it is still active, so that a subscription cancelled or refunded outside the app ends in the app too.
  • RevenueCat acts as our processor. It receives none of your training data — no sets, body weight, height or plans. Its privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide what you bought and as tax and accounting law requires. To ask for them to be deleted, write to the address below.

Your phone's calendar

Off by default. If you turn it on, the app creates a calendar of its own named "Double Progression" and writes your planned sessions into it, and reads back whether you moved or deleted one. It touches only events it created itself. Nothing from your calendar is sent anywhere — it is read and written on the device and nowhere else.

Reminders

Notifications are scheduled locally by your phone. No notification service of ours is involved and nothing is sent over the network.

Android system backup

If your phone has Android's own backup switched on, the app's settings and training data are included in it, so they come back when you set up a new phone. This backup is made by Android into your Google account, is end-to-end encrypted with your screen-lock PIN, pattern or password, and is created only on phones that support that encryption. Neither we nor Google can read it. You control it in your phone's backup settings.

What the app never does

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No crash-reporting service
  • No user accounts on our side; we run no server that holds your data
  • No sale of data, ever. The only outside services involved are Google Play and RevenueCat, and only for purchases as described above
  • No location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo access

Retention and deletion

We retain no training data, because we receive none. Purchase records are described under "Buying Plus" above. Data on your device lasts until you delete it in the app or uninstall Double Progression. A Google Drive backup lasts until you delete it from your own Google account — in your Google account settings, under the connected apps' data.

Children

Double Progression is not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to collect anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be stated in the app.

Čo môže opustiť telefón — a len keď o to požiadaš

Záloha na tvoj Google Disk

Predvolene vypnuté. Keď to zapneš, appka ťa prihlási cez Google a zapíše kópiu tvojich tréningových dát na tvoj vlastný Google Disk, do skrytého priečinka aplikácie (appDataFolder).

  • Žiada sa oprávnenie drive.appdata — najužšie, aké Google Disk ponúka. Appka nevidí, nečíta a ani nevypíše žiadny iný súbor na tvojom Disku.
  • Záloha leží na tvojom Disku, pod tvojím účtom. Nemáme server ani kópiu a nevieme si ju prečítať.
  • Tvoja e-mailová adresa sa v appke zobrazí, aby si vedel, ktorý účet je pripojený. Drží sa len v pamäti, neukladá sa a nikam sa neposiela.
  • Kedykoľvek sa dá odpojiť a zálohu zmazať v nastaveniach svojho Google účtu.

Kúpa Plus (nákupy v aplikácii)

Len ak otvoríš obrazovku Plus, kúpiš alebo obnovíš nákup, alebo máš aktívne predplatné Plus. Kto nič z toho neurobí, s týmito službami sa appka nikdy nespojí.

  • Google Play spracúva platbu. Údaje o karte a fakturácii dostane len Google, my ich nikdy nevidíme. Platia pre ne zásady Googlu.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) za nás overuje nákupy a predplatné, lebo appka nemá vlastný server. Dostane náhodné anonymné ID, ktoré vytvorí appka (nie tvoje meno, e-mail ani účet Google), údaje o nákupe a predplatnom (produkt, cena, mena, dátumy, číslo objednávky), základné technické údaje (verzia appky, verzia Androidu, krajina a jazyk obchodu) a — ako pri každom spojení — tvoju IP adresu.
  • Ak máš predplatné, appka sa pri spustení RevenueCatu opýta, či stále platí, aby sa predplatné zrušené alebo vrátené mimo appky skončilo aj v appke.
  • RevenueCat je náš sprostredkovateľ. Tvoje tréningové dáta nedostane — žiadne série, telesnú hmotnosť, výšku ani plány. Jeho zásady: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Záznamy o nákupoch sa uchovávajú, kým sú potrebné na poskytnutie toho, čo si kúpil, a ako vyžadujú daňové a účtovné predpisy. O ich zmazanie môžeš požiadať na adrese nižšie.

Kalendár v telefóne

Predvolene vypnuté. Keď to zapneš, appka si založí vlastný kalendár „Double Progression", zapíše doň naplánované tréningy a spätne si prečíta, či si niektorý presunul alebo zmazal. Siaha len na udalosti, ktoré sama vytvorila. Z tvojho kalendára nič nikam neodchádza.

Nevieme si ju prečítať my ani Google. Ovládaš ju v nastaveniach zálohy v telefóne.
