Privacy Policy

Double Progression · last updated 28 October 2026

Your training stays on your phone. There is no account to create, no advertising, and no server of ours that your training is sent to. Your sets, body data and notes never leave your phone unless you yourself switch on a backup. The app does send anonymous usage statistics (which screens are opened, where people stop), which you can turn off in settings, and purchases of Plus are processed by Google Play and verified by RevenueCat — see below.

This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device and what does not. It covers the Double Progression mobile app for Android and iOS, published by Martin Lancarič.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your phone's private app storage. It is not transmitted anywhere, it is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you uninstall Double Progression.

What can leave your device — and only if you ask

Backup to your own Google Drive

Off by default. If you turn it on, the app signs you in with Google and writes a copy of your training data to your own Google Drive, into the hidden application folder (appDataFolder).

  • The scope requested is drive.appdata — the narrowest Google Drive offers. The app cannot see, list or read any other file in your Drive.
  • The backup is in your Drive, under your Google account. We have no server and no copy of it. We cannot read it.
  • Your Google e-mail address is shown in the app so you know which account is connected. It is held in memory only and is not stored or transmitted to us.
  • You can disconnect at any time, and delete the backup from your Google account's app-data settings.

A backup made entirely into your own cloud storage, which the developer cannot access, is not collection of your data by us — and it is not declared as such in the Google Play Data safety section.

Buying Plus (in-app purchases)

Only if you open the Plus screen, buy or restore a purchase, or have an active Plus subscription. Anyone who never does any of this never contacts these services.

  • Google Play handles the payment. Your card and billing details go to Google only; we never see them. Google's own privacy policy applies.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) verifies purchases and subscriptions for us, because the app has no server of its own. It receives a random anonymous ID created by the app (not your name, e-mail or Google account), your purchase and subscription details (product, price, currency, dates, order ID), basic technical information (app version, Android version, store country and language) and, as with any connection, your IP address.
  • If you bought Plus (a subscription or for life), the app checks with RevenueCat when it starts whether it is still valid, so that a subscription cancelled or a purchase refunded outside the app ends in the app too.
  • RevenueCat acts as our processor. It receives none of your training data — no sets, body weight, height or plans. Its privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide what you bought and as tax and accounting law requires. To ask for them to be deleted, write to the address below.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

On by default; you can turn it off at any time in the app's settings (Usage statistics). It helps us see where people get stuck — for example how many finish the intro, build a plan or complete their first workout.

  • What is sent: app events such as "intro page 2 viewed", "plan generator opened", "workout started / finished", "Plus screen opened"; the few settings needed to read them (number of training days and minutes chosen in the plan generator, goal and experience level, name of a ready-made plan); and technical data collected automatically by Firebase — a random app-instance ID, device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country/region derived from the IP address, and time spent in the app.
  • What is never sent: your logged sets, weights, body weight, height, sex, notes, the content of your plans, your name or e-mail.
  • The advertising ID is not collected, ad personalisation is off, Google signals are off and the data is not linked to Google Ads.
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC process the data as our processor. It may be transferred to the USA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Data is kept for 14 months. Google's privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by switching the statistics off; turning it off also deletes events not yet sent from your phone.

Your phone's calendar

Off by default. If you turn it on, the app writes your planned sessions into a calendar on your phone — by default the calendar of your Google account, or another one you pick — and reads back whether you moved or deleted one. It touches only events it created itself, which it recognises by a short tag at the end of the event description. The app sends nothing from your calendar to us. If the chosen calendar belongs to an account such as Google, that account's own calendar sync copies the events to its servers, as it does with any event you add yourself.

Reminders

Notifications are scheduled locally by your phone. No notification service of ours is involved and nothing is sent over the network.

Android system backup

If your phone has Android's own backup switched on, the app's settings and training data are included in it, so they come back when you set up a new phone. This backup is made by Android into your Google account, is end-to-end encrypted with your screen-lock PIN, pattern or password, and is created only on phones that support that encryption. Neither we nor Google can read it. You control it in your phone's backup settings.

What the app never does

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No crash-reporting service
  • No user accounts on our side; we run no server that holds your data
  • No sale of data, ever. The only outside services involved are Google Play and RevenueCat for purchases, and Firebase for usage statistics, as described above
  • No location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo access

Retention and deletion

We retain no training data, because we receive none. Usage statistics are kept for 14 months. Purchase records are described under "Buying Plus" above. Data on your device lasts until you delete it in the app or uninstall Double Progression. A Google Drive backup lasts until you delete it from your own Google account — in your Google account settings, under the connected apps' data.

Deleting your data

To ask for your data to be deleted, e-mail matolanco@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". You do not need an account and you do not have to give a reason. We reply within 30 days.

  • Training data (sets, body weight, plans, notes) is only on your phone: delete it in the app or uninstall the app. A Google Drive backup is deleted in your Google account settings, under the connected apps' data.
  • Usage statistics: switch off Usage statistics in the app's settings — this stops collection immediately and deletes events not yet sent. Events already sent are not linked to your name or e-mail; they are deleted automatically after 14 months. If you send us the request, we also ask Google to delete the data linked to your app installation.
  • Purchases of Plus: on request we delete your record at RevenueCat. Payment records that tax and accounting law requires us or Google to keep are kept for the period that law sets.

Children

Double Progression is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to collect anything new, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be stated in the app.

28 October 2026: added "Usage statistics" — anonymous app events via Google Analytics for Firebase, which can be turned off in settings. Training data still never leaves the phone.

Čo môže opustiť telefón — a len keď o to požiadaš

Záloha na tvoj Google Disk

Predvolene vypnuté. Keď to zapneš, appka ťa prihlási cez Google a zapíše kópiu tvojich tréningových dát na tvoj vlastný Google Disk, do skrytého priečinka aplikácie (appDataFolder).

  • Žiada sa oprávnenie drive.appdata — najužšie, aké Google Disk ponúka. Appka nevidí, nečíta a ani nevypíše žiadny iný súbor na tvojom Disku.
  • Záloha leží na tvojom Disku, pod tvojím účtom. Nemáme server ani kópiu a nevieme si ju prečítať.
  • Tvoja e-mailová adresa sa v appke zobrazí, aby si vedel, ktorý účet je pripojený. Drží sa len v pamäti, neukladá sa a nikam sa neposiela.
  • Kedykoľvek sa dá odpojiť a zálohu zmazať v nastaveniach svojho Google účtu.

Kúpa Plus (nákupy v aplikácii)

Len ak otvoríš obrazovku Plus, kúpiš alebo obnovíš nákup, alebo máš aktívne predplatné Plus. Kto nič z toho neurobí, s týmito službami sa appka nikdy nespojí.

  • Google Play spracúva platbu. Údaje o karte a fakturácii dostane len Google, my ich nikdy nevidíme. Platia pre ne zásady Googlu.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) za nás overuje nákupy a predplatné, lebo appka nemá vlastný server. Dostane náhodné anonymné ID, ktoré vytvorí appka (nie tvoje meno, e-mail ani účet Google), údaje o nákupe a predplatnom (produkt, cena, mena, dátumy, číslo objednávky), základné technické údaje (verzia appky, verzia Androidu, krajina a jazyk obchodu) a — ako pri každom spojení — tvoju IP adresu.
  • Ak si Plus kúpil (predplatné aj navždy), appka sa pri spustení RevenueCatu opýta, či stále platí, aby sa predplatné zrušené alebo nákup vrátený mimo appky skončil aj v appke.
  • RevenueCat je náš sprostredkovateľ. Tvoje tréningové dáta nedostane — žiadne série, telesnú hmotnosť, výšku ani plány. Jeho zásady: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Záznamy o nákupoch sa uchovávajú, kým sú potrebné na poskytnutie toho, čo si kúpil, a ako vyžadujú daňové a účtovné predpisy. O ich zmazanie môžeš požiadať na adrese nižšie.

Štatistiky používania (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Predvolene zapnuté; kedykoľvek ich vypneš v nastaveniach appky (Štatistiky používania). Pomáhajú nám zistiť, kde sa ľudia zasekávajú — napríklad koľkí dokončia úvod, zostavia si plán alebo dokončia prvý tréning.

  • Čo sa posiela: udalosti v appke, napr. „zobrazená 2. strana úvodu", „otvorený sprievodca plánom", „tréning začatý / dokončený", „otvorená obrazovka Plus"; pár volieb potrebných na ich vyhodnotenie (počet tréningových dní a minút zo sprievodcu, cieľ a skúsenosť, názov hotového plánu); a technické údaje, ktoré Firebase zbiera sám — náhodný identifikátor inštalácie, model zariadenia, verzia Androidu, verzia appky, jazyk, približná krajina/región odvodená z IP adresy a čas strávený v appke.
  • Čo sa neposiela nikdy: zapísané série, váhy, telesná hmotnosť, výška, pohlavie, poznámky, obsah tvojich plánov, meno ani e-mail.
  • Reklamný identifikátor sa nezbiera, reklamná personalizácia je vypnutá, Google signals sú vypnuté a údaje sa neprepájajú s Google Ads.
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC údaje spracúvajú ako náš sprostredkovateľ. Môžu sa preniesť do USA v rámci EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Uchovávajú sa 14 mesiacov. Informácie od Google: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Právny základ: náš oprávnený záujem zlepšovať appku (čl. 6 ods. 1 písm. f) GDPR). Namietať môžeš kedykoľvek vypnutím štatistík; vypnutie zmaže aj udalosti, ktoré ešte neodišli z telefónu.
