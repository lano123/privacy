Privacy Policy

Treevium · last updated 21 September 2026

Treevium does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours that your training is sent to. Everything the app records stays on your phone unless you yourself switch on a backup.

This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device and what does not. It covers the Treevium mobile app for Android and iOS, published by Matúš Lancarič.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your phone's private app storage. It is not transmitted anywhere, it is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you uninstall Treevium.

WhatWhy the app keeps it
Logged sets — exercise, weight, repetitions, optional RPE, dateProgress tracking, next-load suggestions, unlocking skill-tree nodes
Body weight history and height, if you enter themBody weight is needed for bodyweight-relative thresholds. Height is stored only as a figure you entered; the app computes nothing from it.

You may delete any of it inside the app, and uninstalling removes all of it.

What can leave your device — and only if you ask

Backup to your own Google Drive

Off by default. If you turn it on, the app signs you in with Google and writes a copy of your training data to your own Google Drive, into the hidden application folder (appDataFolder).

  • The scope requested is drive.appdata — the narrowest Google Drive offers. The app cannot see, list or read any other file in your Drive.
  • The backup is in your Drive, under your Google account. We have no server and no copy of it. We cannot read it.
  • Your Google e-mail address is shown in the app so you know which account is connected. It is held in memory only and is not stored or transmitted to us.
  • You can disconnect at any time, and delete the backup from your Google account's app-data settings.

A backup made entirely into your own cloud storage, which the developer cannot access, is not collection of your data by us — and it is not declared as such in the Google Play Data safety section.

Your phone's calendar

Off by default. If you turn it on, the app creates a calendar of its own named "Treevium" and writes your planned sessions into it, and reads back whether you moved or deleted one. It touches only events it created itself. Nothing from your calendar is sent anywhere — it is read and written on the device and nowhere else.

Reminders

Notifications are scheduled locally by your phone. No notification service of ours is involved and nothing is sent over the network.

What the app never does

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No crash-reporting service
  • No user accounts on our side; we run no server that holds your data
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties, because we hold none
  • No location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo access

Retention and deletion

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data on your device lasts until you delete it in the app or uninstall Treevium. A Google Drive backup lasts until you delete it from your own Google account — in your Google account settings, under the connected apps' data.

Children

Treevium is not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to collect anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be stated in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything in this policy: matolanco@gmail.com

Zásady ochrany osobných údajov

Treevium o tebe nezbiera žiadne údaje. Nie je tu účet, ktorý by si si zakladal, žiadne analytiky, reklama ani sledovanie a žiadny náš server, na ktorý by tvoje tréningy odchádzali. Všetko, čo si appka zapíše, zostáva v telefóne — pokiaľ si sám nezapneš zálohu.

Tieto zásady sa týkajú mobilnej aplikácie Treevium pre Android a iOS, ktorú vydáva Matúš Lancarič.

Čo môže opustiť telefón — a len keď o to požiadaš

Záloha na tvoj Google Disk

Predvolene vypnuté. Keď to zapneš, appka ťa prihlási cez Google a zapíše kópiu tvojich tréningových dát na tvoj vlastný Google Disk, do skrytého priečinka aplikácie (appDataFolder).

  • Žiada sa oprávnenie drive.appdata — najužšie, aké Google Disk ponúka. Appka nevidí, nečíta a ani nevypíše žiadny iný súbor na tvojom Disku.
  • Záloha leží na tvojom Disku, pod tvojím účtom. Nemáme server ani kópiu a nevieme si ju prečítať.
  • Tvoja e-mailová adresa sa v appke zobrazí, aby si vedel, ktorý účet je pripojený. Drží sa len v pamäti, neukladá sa a nikam sa neposiela.
  • Kedykoľvek sa dá odpojiť a zálohu zmazať v nastaveniach svojho Google účtu.

Kalendár v telefóne

Predvolene vypnuté. Keď to zapneš, appka si založí vlastný kalendár „Treevium", zapíše doň naplánované tréningy a spätne si prečíta, či si niektorý presunul alebo zmazal. Siaha len na udalosti, ktoré sama vytvorila. Z tvojho kalendára nič nikam neodchádza.

Pripomienky

Notifikácie plánuje priamo tvoj telefón. Žiadna naša služba pri tom nie je a po sieti sa neposiela nič.

Čo appka nerobí nikdy

  • Žiadne analytiky ani sledovanie používania
  • Žiadna reklama a žiadne reklamné identifikátory
  • Žiadna služba na hlásenie pádov
  • Žiadne účty na našej strane; neprevádzkujeme server, na ktorom by tvoje dáta ležali
  • Žiadny predaj ani zdieľanie údajov tretím stranám — nemáme čo zdieľať
  • Žiadny prístup k polohe, kontaktom, mikrofónu, fotoaparátu ani fotkám

Uchovávanie a mazanie

Neuchovávame nič, lebo nič nedostávame. Dáta v telefóne trvajú, kým ich nezmažeš v appke alebo appku neodinštaluješ. Záloha na Google Disku trvá, kým ju nezmažeš vo svojom Google účte — v nastaveniach pripojených aplikácií a ich dát.
